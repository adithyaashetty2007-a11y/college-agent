COLLEGE INFORMATION

College Name: St Joseph Engineering College (SJEC)

Departments:
- Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
- Information Science and Engineering (ISE)
- Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
- Mechanical Engineering
- Civil Engineering
- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

CSE Department:
The Computer Science and Engineering department is located in the main academic block.

CSE department provides education in programming, data structures, algorithms,
database systems, computer networks, operating systems, artificial intelligence,
machine learning and software development.

Students can contact the CSE department office for academic information,
faculty information and department-related queries.
